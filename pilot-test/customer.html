Get help now — first reply, not a booking

  • Tell us what's wrong — speak or type it.
  • You'll get a first reply when the service responds — up to about 2 minutes.
  • Not a confirmed booking yet — a tradesperson confirms the price and time.
If you smell gas, do not wait for a reply. Open windows, turn off the gas meter valve if you can do so safely, do not flick electrical switches, leave the property if you are worried, and call the National Gas Emergency number 0800 111 999 straight away.

Speaking is a first option, not an extra — if you find it easier to talk than type, use Speak. If you prefer typing, type. Both work. On iPhone/Android you can also use the keyboard mic.

Spoken reply uses your browser's built-in voice, if available. Voice input may be processed by your browser's speech service — it is not claimed to always stay on-device. Tap Stop to stop. Tap Hear again to replay.

Prefer to talk to a person?
